bridge ontology parameterdp back to ToC or Data Property ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/bedro#BridgeOntologyParameter

Parameter following the Bridge Ontology Parametercoding (BOP) convention, developed at the Institute of Concrete Structures, Technische Universität Dresden. The parameters define geometric and other attributes of bridge components. Each parameter is coded with a ten-character alphanumeric code: AAAbbCCdEE (A: component, b: transversal position, C: sub-component, d: attribute type, E: attribute number). X or 00 means global [applies for every (sub-)component/ attribute of that kind].
